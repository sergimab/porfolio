10 uses of augmented reality

Surgery in detail

Using tablets or holographic glasses, surgeons can visualise organs in 3D or check the patient's history before or during the procedure.

Cars built to order

More and more car makers let potential buyers visualise their future car and customise it to their taste and needs.

Lessons come to life

Many students already use notebooks with special markers that, held up to a computer or mobile screen, react by showing 3D images.

Attention, athletes

Runners, cyclists, climbers and hikers can now explore their routes projected onto any surface in 3D, plan their next adventure and share their activities.

Instant translation

Perfect for travellers. Just take a photo of any text in front of you (a report, an ad, a recipe...) and get an instant translation.

Facial scanning

It sounds like science fiction, but there's already a service that can recognise a person's face and, at the same time, show the social networks they're on.

Smart architecture

Many companies use AR so clients can see in real time how their home will look once the works are finished, or how a new sofa will fit with the rest of the decor.

Perfect for advertising

From virtually trying on clothes to watching an interactive campaign on a wall in the middle of the street. The sales and promotional potential AR offers brands is huge.

The universe up close

Point your phone's camera at the sky and identify stars, constellations, planets and other celestial bodies, along with extra information about them.

Only ‘fit’ for NASA

Using smart glasses, astronauts now have the technology to receive visual instructions if repairs are needed during their missions.
