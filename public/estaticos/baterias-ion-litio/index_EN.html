Tips to extend the lifespan of lithium-ion batteries

Minimise exposure to high temperatures during use or storage.

Minimise use at low temperatures, especially while charging.

Minimise time spent charged at 100%.

Minimise time spent discharged at 0%.

Avoid fast charging unless necessary.

Avoid discharging the device faster than needed.

Avoid use or storage in humid environments.

Follow the manufacturer's calibration instructions.

Source: University of Michigan
